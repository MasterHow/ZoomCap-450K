# NOTICE

This repository contains **tooling and documentation only**. It contains no
caption corpus, and no images of any kind.

## The data

The ZoomCap-450K caption corpus is published separately on HuggingFace under
**CC BY-NC 4.0** (Attribution-NonCommercial), research use only:
https://huggingface.co/datasets/MasterHow/ZoomCap-450K

## Provenance and what is deliberately absent

* **No images are redistributed.** Every row carries the image key under which the
  picture is published upstream, in
  [ScaleCap-450k](https://huggingface.co/datasets/long-xing1/ScaleCap-450k)
  (`images` + `conversations`, CC BY-NC 4.0), whose image pool is ShareGPT4V-100K
  plus a LAION-5B subset. Obtaining the images remains the user's responsibility
  under those upstream terms.
* The row schema of this corpus is identical to ScaleCap-450k's on purpose, so the
  corpus is a drop-in caption replacement for that one.
* Captions are model-generated by the ZoomCap evidence-routing workflow (teacher:
  Qwen3.5-35B-A3B) and may contain hallucinated detail. Not for medical, legal,
  diagnostic, identification or surveillance use.
